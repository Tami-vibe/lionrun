# SEO and AI-Era Discoverability

- Generate crawlable server-rendered or static HTML for published sites; do not require JavaScript for primary content.
- Support unique titles, meta descriptions, canonical URLs, robots directives, Open Graph metadata, semantic headings, alt text, and internal links.
- Generate valid XML sitemaps, robots.txt, correct 404/410 status codes, and redirects where applicable.
- Support structured data appropriate to verified page content (e.g., Organization, LocalBusiness, Product, Service, BreadcrumbList, FAQ where eligible). Validate syntax and content consistency; do not fabricate reviews, prices, or claims.
- Offer editable business identity, contact details, hours, service area, address, and location pages when relevant.
- Keep navigation and content accessible to crawlers; minimize duplicate/thin pages and broken links.
- For AI-assisted search, prioritize factual, structured, cited/traceable business information and clear page semantics. No platform can guarantee AI citations or ranking.
- Treat AI-generated copy and schema as drafts requiring validation and user approval; never invent business facts.
- Verify published HTML, status codes, canonical targets, sitemap entries, and schema output using automated checks and actual HTTP responses.
- Monitor indexing/search performance where the customer connects relevant services; do not promise rankings.
